    Amazon Search, Category & Seller Listings Extractor is an Apify Actor published by Dataspryva. It accepts 20 input fields, none of them required at the top level. Each result can carry up to 8 declared fields, including title, asin, price, stars, reviewsCount and inStock. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Pull Amazon product listings from keyword search, category/search URLs, browse-node IDs, seller storefronts, ASINs, and exact product URLs in one run. Every record carries the listing card data — price, brand, rating, badges, images, delivery hints, and organic/sponsored position — and optional product-detail enrichment adds specifications, the full gallery, seller identity, rating histogram, variants, and bestseller ranks. No login, cookies, or API key needed.

    What this actor does

    • Six additive input modes. Combine keywords, categoryUrls, categoryNodeIds, sellerIds, asins, and productUrls in the same run; every source is processed and deduplicated by ASIN.
    • Exact product lookup. ASIN and product-URL modes open /dp/{ASIN} directly. They never fall back to an Amazon keyword search, so a known product cannot turn into an unrelated result.
    • Real category listings. Node IDs use Amazon's /s?rh=n:{node}&fs=true listing route, not the /b?node= merchandising landing page. Search, seller (/s?me=), and seller-profile (/sp?seller=) URLs are routed automatically.
    • Playwright reliability. Each marketplace session is warmed up on the homepage and paced with human-like jitter. Continue-shopping interstitials, dog pages, rate limits, and interstitial challenges are classified and retried. Proxy selection is automatic: an available non-residential (datacenter/static) group in the marketplace's country, never residential by default.
    • Listing intelligence. Brand, card image set and large image, organic position, sponsored flag, ships-to line, variant count, unit price, deal text, stock hint, sold-by line, and a genuine list price kept separate from Amazon's "Typical price".
    • Detail enrichment. Specifications, gallery images, rating histogram, reviews link, seller ID/name/URL, variant dimensions, BSR, breadcrumbs, description, features, and stock status.
    • Safe output. Empty fields are omitted at every depth; nothing is emitted as null.

    Use cases

    • Competitor price tracking — monitor price, list price, typical price, and deal evidence.
    • Category and keyword research — collect top products with brand, rating, and organic rank.
    • Sponsored-placement analysis — compare organic position with sponsored rows.
    • Seller storefront audits — enumerate a seller's listed products.
    • Product research — pull full specifications, gallery, variants, and BSR for a shortlist.
    • Multi-marketplace checks — compare the same ASIN or keyword across 21 storefronts.

    Questions

    Do I need Amazon cookies or an API key?

    No. The actor reads public search, category, seller, and product pages anonymously. No account, cookie, token, or API key is required.

    Do ASIN inputs still search Amazon?

    No. ASIN and product-URL modes fetch the exact /dp/{ASIN} page. Keyword mode is the only mode that performs a search.

    Why is listPrice missing on a product that shows a "Typical price"?

    "Typical price" is not a strike-through list price. It is returned separately as typicalPrice, and discountPercent is calculated only from a genuine current price plus list price. This prevents fake discounts.

    Why are Prime or sponsored fields missing from some records?

    Amazon does not render Prime icons or sponsored labels for every marketplace, exit country, or layout. The actor reports only what the page actually shows and never claims a product is "not Prime" from a missing badge.

    Why do I get fewer results than maxPages suggests?

    Amazon caps keyword search at page 7. Category listings can go deeper. For more than a keyword surface allows, split the query by category, brand, or price band and combine the runs.

    Does the actor need my proxy?

    No. Proxy selection is automatic: it uses an available non-residential Apify Proxy group in the marketplace's own country and never switches to residential billing on its own. There is no proxy input to configure.

    Why do prices appear in a currency different from the marketplace domain?

    Amazon localizes currency and delivery to the request's exit country. The actor sets the marketplace country automatically and reports the resolved currency; cross-border renders can still differ from the domain's nominal currency.

    How expensive is detail enrichment?

    Each detail page is a full Amazon product page (roughly 2.5-3.1 MB). Set scrapeProductDetails to false for large listing runs and enable it only for a shortlist or for ASIN/product-URL modes.

    Is this affiliated with Amazon?

    No. It is an independent third-party actor that reads Amazon's public pages.
