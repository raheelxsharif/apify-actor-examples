Public posts and profiles need no login; add your own cookies for age-restricted posts and private accounts you follow. Each file gets a download link, size, resolution and length.

    Instagram Downloader API - Reels, Video & Photo is an Apify Actor published by Dataspryva. It accepts 6 input fields, none of them required at the top level. Each result can carry up to 11 declared fields, including status, filename, post_url, username, type and download_status. It costs $0.49 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.30 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Save the actual image and video files behind Instagram posts, Reels, IGTV videos and carousels. Paste post links, list a few usernames, or do both, and each file is stored in your run's key-value store with a ready-to-use download link. The dataset adds a record for every file with its source post, owner, file type, dimensions, size and, for videos, length, frame rate and bitrate. It is built for people who need a clean local copy of public Instagram media together with the details needed to catalogue it.

    What this actor does

    • Downloads from post links: accepts /p/, /reel/, /reels/ and /tv/ URLs, with or without the username in the path
    • Downloads from usernames: finds the most recent posts and Reels on each profile (up to 100 per profile) and downloads them
    • Splits carousels: every photo or clip in a multi-item post becomes its own file and its own record
    • Describes every file: width, height, aspect ratio, extension and size, plus duration, frame rate, codec and bitrate for videos
    • Gives you a download link: each file is saved under a stable key with a public URL you can open or fetch from your own tools

    Use cases

    • Social media managers: keep an offline backup of your brand's own posts and Reels before editing or deleting them
    • Content and creative teams: collect user-generated photos and clips from a campaign for review and rights clearance
    • Digital archivists and librarians: preserve public cultural, news or institutional accounts together with file-level details
    • Academics and journalists: build a media sample from named public accounts for research or analysis
    • Video editors and agencies: pull reference footage and measure resolution, duration and bitrate before planning edits
    • Competitive analysts: review the visual style and format mix (photo, carousel, Reel) of rival accounts

    The publisher's output notes

    Output per media file

    One dataset record is created per downloaded file. A carousel with 5 items produces 5 records. A post that could not be downloaded produces one short record with only its post_url and a status that names the cause. The explanation, which post it was and what you can do about it, is in the run log, one line per post, followed by a closing Done: summary. While the run is going, the status message of the run shows a live count such as Downloaded 12/20 posts - 2 need a sign-in.

    • status – success for a downloaded file, otherwise the reason it failed (see the status table below)
    • filename – saved file name including extension, named after the media ID (e.g. 3918833148741120108.jpg)
    • post_url – the Instagram post the file came from
    • username – Instagram handle of the post owner
    • type – image or video (unknown if the file format is not recognised)
    • download_status – finished once the file has been saved
    • downloaded_at – timestamp of the download
    • storage_key – key of the file in the key-value store
    • download_url – public link to the stored file
    • media_meta_data – technical details of the file:
      • width, height – size in pixels
      • aspect_ratio – width divided by height, rounded to 2 decimals
      • ext – file extension (jpg, mp4, ...)
      • filesize_bytes – size on disk in bytes
      • fps – frames per second (videos only)
      • duration – length in seconds (videos only)
      • video_codec – codec identifier such as avc1 (videos only)
      • total_bitrate_kbps – overall bitrate in kilobits per second (videos only)

    Any technical detail that cannot be determined for a file is returned as null.

    Questions

    Do I need an Instagram account or cookies to use this actor?

    No. Public posts need no sign-in, and neither do the posts of a public username. Only age-restricted posts and private accounts need cookies, and you can paste your own in the optional cookies field.

    Do I need to set up a proxy?

    No. The actor chooses a working connection to Instagram automatically. Just fill in the input and run it.

    Can I download from private accounts?

    Yes, with your own cookies from an account that follows the private account. This works for a username (its posts are listed in a signed-in browser) and for a post link. Without cookies, or with cookies of an account that does not follow it, the dataset gets a record with the status post_is_private or profile_is_private and no media.

    Are carousel posts supported?

    Yes. Each photo or video in a carousel is downloaded separately and gets its own dataset record and its own key-value store entry, so a 5-item carousel gives you 5 files.

    What happens if I set maxPostsperusername to 0?

    No posts are collected from the usernames. Links in postUrls are still downloaded. Use this when you only want to download specific posts.

    Where do I find the downloaded files?

    Open the run's key-value store: every file is saved there under the storage_key shown in its dataset record. The download_url field gives a direct link to the same file.

    Why do some videos have empty technical details?

    Fields such as codec or bitrate are measured from the saved file. If a value cannot be determined, it comes back as null while the file itself is still downloaded.

    Can I download Stories or highlights?

    No. Only posts, Reels and IGTV videos are supported.

    How fresh is the data?

    Every run reads the posts live, so files and details reflect each post as it is at the time of the run.

    Is this affiliated with Instagram or Meta?

    No. This is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Instagram or Meta Platforms, Inc.

    Limits and notes

    Authentication

    Public posts are downloaded without signing in, and so are the posts of any public profile, however many you ask for: they are listed the way Instagram lists them for a visitor who is not signed in. A profile is always read that way first, even when you give cookies. Only a signed-in view can open posts behind an age check and private accounts your account follows; for those, paste your own Instagram cookies into the optional cookies field (see Using your own cookies below). Without cookies those cases are reported with their status. The actor does not sign in with any account of its own.

    Using your own cookies

    Cookies are optional and only used for what a signed-out visitor cannot see. To add them, export your instagram.com cookies as JSON from a browser where you are signed in, using any cookie-export browser extension, and paste the JSON into the cookies field. The field is a secret input, so the Apify Console hides and encrypts it, and the cookies are used only during your run. Before anything is downloaded with them, the actor asks Instagram whether it recognises the cookies; if they are invalid, expired or signed out, the run log says so and the affected posts are reported without a download. Only paste cookies from an account that belongs to you, and only collect what you are allowed to access. You are responsible for complying with Instagram's terms and applicable law, and a dedicated account is the safer choice.

    Cookie format

    The cookies field takes a JSON array of cookie objects, the format most cookie-export browser extensions call "JSON". Other export formats are not supported: a Netscape cookies.txt file or a raw Cookie: header string will be rejected. Each cookie needs at least name, value and domain, and the export must include the sessionid and ds_user_id cookies of the signed-in account. A cookie that has an expiry date (expirationDate, in seconds since 1970) must not be expired.

    Known limitations

    • Private accounts need cookies: a private username or a post from a private account opens only with the cookies of an account that follows it. Without cookies, or with cookies of an account that does not follow it, you receive a record with the status post_is_private (a post) or profile_is_private (a username) instead.
    • No Stories: only permanent posts, Reels and IGTV videos are downloaded.
    • Stored files expire: files live in the run's key-value store and follow your Apify plan's data retention. Export anything you need to keep for the long term.
    • Profile depth: a profile yields at most 100 posts per run, and the number actually available depends on how many posts Instagram shows for that profile.

    Responsible use

    Instagram posts that anyone can see can still show or identify real people, and the files this actor saves (faces, voices, locations, captions burned into images) are personal data in many cases. Before downloading, make sure you have a lawful basis (for example under the GDPR) for keeping and processing that media, limit what you keep to what you need, and honour requests from the people shown. Do not use downloaded media to profile, track or target minors, or to single out people by special categories of personal data such as health, religion, political opinion or sexual orientation.

    Photos and videos downloaded by this actor belong to their creators. Make sure you have the rights or permission for any reuse.
