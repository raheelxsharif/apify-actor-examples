    Finn.no Marketplace, Vehicles, Property & Jobs Extractor is an Apify Actor published by Dataspryva. It accepts 148 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 6 declared fields, including adId, heading, vertical, price, location and adUrl. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Scrape Finn.no — Norway's largest online classifieds marketplace with millions of listings across items for sale, cars, motorcycles, boats, trucks & trailers, buses, construction machinery, agriculture equipment, caravans, motorhomes, real estate (for sale, for rent, holiday homes, plots, commercial, businesses for sale), and jobs. Choose from 24 search modes to target one specific vertical with its own rich filter set (price, year, mileage, location, and more), fan out across the 5 highest-volume categories in a single run with searchAll, or fetch complete listing details from any Finn.no ad URL. HTTP-only — no login, cookies, API key, or proxy required.

    What this actor does

    • 24 search modes — items, cars, motorcycles & mopeds, boats, trucks & trailers, buses, construction machinery, agriculture equipment, caravans, motorhomes, 11 real-estate sub-verticals, and jobs, each with its own filters and output fields
    • Full listing details — searchByUrls fetches description, address/coordinates, vehicle specifications, property facilities and broker contact, or employer/employment data from any listing URL; includeDetails enriches search results with the same data
    • Coordinates on every listing — search results include latitude/longitude when Finn.no publishes a map pin
    • searchAll fan-out — one run across items, cars, real estate for sale, real estate for rent, and jobs — the 5 highest-volume categories
    • Deep per-vertical filters — make/model-adjacent enums, condition/fuel/transmission dropdowns, equipment & amenity checklists, and year/mileage/price/power/area range filters
    • Sort & scope controls — relevance, newest/oldest, price, or distance; filter by Norwegian county (fylke) and "published today"
    • Empty fields are omitted — only fields with real data are returned; nothing comes back as null

    Use cases

    • Resale & arbitrage — track newly listed items and price movements to spot resale opportunities
    • Car dealerships & buyers — monitor the used-car market for specific makes, fuel types, or price bands
    • Real estate agencies & investors — watch new for-sale or rental listings in a target county or neighbourhood
    • Recruiters & job boards — aggregate open positions by industry, sector, or work arrangement
    • Market researchers & pricing analysts — build cross-category snapshots of NOK pricing trends with searchAll
    • Equipment & fleet buyers — source used trucks, construction machinery, or agriculture equipment by year and engine power

    Only fields with real data are included — empty/unavailable fields are omitted rather than returned as null. A real 0 (e.g. a sailboat with no engine) is always kept; only truly absent values are omitted.

    Note on condition vs. the itemCondition input filter: these are different concepts. itemCondition narrows search results by the item's physical condition (brand new, like new, well used, etc.) — a search-only facet Finn.no doesn't echo back per listing. The condition output field reflects whether the ad is a sale, a free giveaway, or a "wanted" post — always available, but a different concept. If you want a filter whose value shows up in the output condition field, use tradeType instead.

    Note on maxItems with no query: requesting a large maxItems (e.g. 1000) with query left empty (browsing all listings in a category) can return fewer records than requested, because Finn.no's query-less browse view doesn't advance monotonically across result pages. When this happens the run's status message reports the shortfall explicitly (e.g. "Scraped 721 listings from Finn.no (fewer than the requested 1000: ...)") instead of silently truncating. Searches with a real query reliably deliver the full requested count.

    Every mode-specific filter above is simply ignored when the selected mode doesn't support it — no error, no crash. When running searchAll, every filter is applied to the vertical(s) it belongs to (e.g. carFuel only affects the cars leg).

    Questions

    What is Finn.no?

    Finn.no is Norway's largest online marketplace, operated by Schibsted Media Group. It hosts millions of listings for items, cars, motorcycles, boats, real estate, jobs, travel, and more.

    Do I need a Finn.no account?

    No. Public listings are accessible without login, cookies, or an API key.

    How are prices returned?

    Prices are in Norwegian Krone (NOK) as integers.

    What does "Search all categories" (searchAll) do?

    It queries items, cars, real estate for sale, real estate for rent, and jobs in the same run and splits maxItems evenly across those five categories, giving a cross-category snapshot in a single run. The niche verticals (motorcycles, boats, the 9 real-estate sub-verticals, and the 8 commercial/RV modes) aren't part of this fan-out — select them individually via mode.

    Does this cover trucks, buses, construction machinery, agriculture equipment, caravans, and motorhomes?

    Yes. searchTrucks, searchBuses, and searchConstructionMachinery cover commercial vehicles. searchAgricultureTractors, searchAgricultureCombines, and searchAgricultureTools cover farm equipment. searchCaravans and searchMotorhomes cover recreational vehicles — each with its own vehicle-group/category filters and year/engine-power/weight/length range filters.

    Does this cover rental properties too, or just homes for sale?

    Both. searchRealEstate covers homes for sale; searchRealEstateRentals covers homes for rent, with its own monthly-rent pricing and furnished-status field. Nine further sub-verticals are available individually: holiday homes for sale, holiday-home plots, property abroad, new-build projects, residential plots, commercial premises for sale/rent, commercial plots, and businesses for sale as a going concern.

    Does this cover motorcycles and boats too, or just cars?

    Yes. searchMc covers motorcycles, mopeds, ATVs, and snowmobiles; searchBoats covers boats — each with its own filter set and output fields (registration number & engine displacement for MC; motor power, length, width, and top speed for boats).

    How many listings can I scrape per run?

    Up to 1,000 using maxItems.

    Can I sort results?

    Yes — by relevance (default), newest/oldest first, price ascending/descending, or distance.

    Can I filter by county, category, condition, fuel type, occupation, energy label, etc.?

    Yes — see the Input table above. Every filter that doesn't apply to the currently selected mode is simply ignored, with no error.

    Are there filters that aren't available?

    A few facets are intentionally left out because Finn.no's underlying value sets are too large or too unstable to keep as a reliable dropdown: item sub-categories (500+ values), item/car brand (up to ~1,800 values), job sub-occupations (~214 values beneath the 84 top-level jobOccupation values), and per-vertical vehicle make on trucks/buses/construction/agriculture/caravans/motorhomes (10–212 values each). Use query for these (e.g. query: "Samsung" or query: "Scania"). Viewing dates and map/radius search are also out of scope since they're rolling/date-based or client-side-only on Finn.no; scheduled viewing times that Finn does return per listing still appear in the viewingTimes output field.

    Why do vehicle year filters (e.g. carYearMax) top out at 2027?

    Finn.no's vehicle year filter stops working correctly once a bound goes past the site's current live ceiling — instead of narrowing results, it silently returns the unfiltered set. The input schema caps at the current live ceiling to avoid that. Finn extends the ceiling by roughly a year whenever the next model year goes on sale; for a newer model year in the meantime, use query (e.g. query: "2028 modell").

    Can I fetch a listing's full description and specifications?

    Yes. Set mode=searchByUrls and pass listing URLs in detailUrls, or set includeDetails=true on any search to enrich every returned record. Detail pages provide the full description, exact coordinates/address, complete vehicle specification list and equipment, property facilities and broker contact, or job employer/employment data. Addresses and contact details are only included when Finn.no publishes them.

    Why do details sometimes take longer?

    searchByUrls and includeDetails=true make one extra request per listing and those requests are paced to stay polite; a 20-listing search therefore takes roughly 20 detail fetches. Search-only runs stay fast because they read the listing data already embedded in the search page.

    Is this affiliated with Finn.no?

    No, this is a third-party actor that reads Finn.no's public search results. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Finn.no or Schibsted Media Group.
