    Remotive Remote Job Listings Extractor is an Apify Actor published by Dataspryva. It accepts 14 input fields, none of them required at the top level. Each result can carry up to 10 declared fields, including title, companyName, category, location, jobType and seniority. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Remotive curates fully remote openings across software, design, marketing, data, support and more. This actor turns its public job catalog into clean, structured records: browse the whole board or search by keyword, then narrow by category, candidate location, employment type, seniority, language and skills. Every record links back to the Remotive listing.

    What this actor does

    • Two modes: browse walks the current catalog; search adds a keyword matched against titles and descriptions.
    • Useful filters: category, candidate location, employment type, seniority, listing language, skills (any or all), posted-within days, salary disclosed, and exclude keywords.
    • Real pagination: the actor follows the public result pages until maxItems is reached or the source runs out, deduplicating by Remotive job ID.
    • Complete records: company, logo, category, location, employment type, seniority, salary text, skill tags, full plain-text description, publication date, expiry date, company website, eligible countries and apply link.
    • No login, no key, no proxy setup.

    Questions

    Do I need a Remotive account or API key?

    No. The actor reads Remotive's public job catalog and public job pages without logging in.

    How many jobs can one run return?

    The public catalog serves result pages of about 50 jobs. The actor follows those pages until maxItems is reached or Remotive stops returning new results, so a browse run can collect several hundred current listings. Very deep result pages are a limited public preview; use category, location and skill filters to reach more of the catalog.

    Why is salary sometimes missing?

    Not every employer publishes pay. When a listing has no salary, the field is omitted instead of filled with a placeholder.

    Is the salary parsed into numbers?

    No. Pay formats vary too much to convert reliably, so the actor returns the employer's salary text unchanged.

    Why do some records lack a description?

    Descriptions come from each job page. If a job page is unavailable during the run, the record keeps its catalog fields and the run status reports how many pages were skipped.

    Does this actor republish Remotive's jobs elsewhere?

    It returns the data to you with a link to the original Remotive listing. Follow Remotive's terms and keep the attribution when you use the data.

    Is this affiliated with Remotive?

    No. This is an independent, third-party actor.

    Can I search by company?

    Put the company name in query; Remotive matches it against titles and descriptions. Company-name filtering is a paid Remotive feature and is not used here.

    What happens if Remotive rate limits the actor?

    The actor retries with backoff and reports a rate-limit status instead of returning an empty result. Wait a minute and run it again.

    How fresh are the listings?

    Each run reads Remotive's current public catalog live. Re-run on a schedule and compare job IDs to spot new postings.
