    Medium Article Discovery & Metadata Extractor is an Apify Actor published by Dataspryva. It accepts 12 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 8 declared fields, including title, authorName, publicationName, publishedDate, readingTimeMinutes and clapCount. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Medium is full of useful writing, but browsing it by hand makes it hard to compare coverage across topics, authors and publications. This actor turns five common discovery paths — tag browsing, author feeds, publication feeds, keyword search and direct article URLs — into one consistent article dataset you can sort, filter and join with your own tools.

    Every record describes a single article: who wrote it, where it was published, when it went live, how long it takes to read, how much engagement it has, whether it sits behind Medium's member paywall, and which topics and tags it carries.

    Questions

    Do I need a Medium account or cookies?

    No. The actor reads publicly available article metadata without logging in.

    Do I need to configure a proxy?

    No. The actor picks a working connection automatically, so you never supply proxy settings, cookies or an API key.

    Can I get the full article text?

    This actor focuses on discovery and metadata. It returns titles, previews, author and publication details and engagement metrics, not the article body.

    How far back can it go?

    It follows the source's own pagination until maxItems is reached or the source runs out. Very large requests can span many pages, so raise maxItems deliberately.

    Why are some fields missing on some records?

    Medium does not expose every field for every article. A personal post may have no publication, an older post may have no subtitle, and some articles have no tags. Missing values are omitted instead of filled with placeholders.

    How are paywalled articles handled?

    isPaywalled marks member-only articles, and excludePaywalled removes them. Only public metadata is collected; the actor never tries to read member-only body content.

    What counts as an article URL?

    Standard Medium links, medium.com/p/... short links, and articles hosted on a custom Medium domain such as a publication's own website.

    Can I combine filters?

    Yes. fromDate, containsKeyword and excludePaywalled work together in every mode.

    Is this affiliated with Medium?

    No. This is an independent third-party actor that reads publicly available article metadata. It is not operated by or affiliated with Medium.

    Limits and notes

    Discovery modes

    Mode What it collects Best for
    byTag Articles tagged with a topic, newest first or by top stories from the week/month/year Trend tracking and editorial research
    byUser An author's articles, including posts distributed to publications Monitoring a writer's output
    byPublication Articles published under a publication Following a publication's latest work
    search Articles matching a keyword query Broad topic discovery
    byUrl Metadata for one or more article URLs Enriching a list of links you already have

    Tag, author, publication and search modes walk the source's pagination until maxItems is reached or the source runs out, so a run can return thousands of records rather than a single page.

    What you can do with it

    • Build a topic monitoring sheet that shows which authors and publications are covering a subject each week.
    • Compare top and newest stories for a tag to separate evergreen hits from fresh coverage.
    • Filter out member-only posts before sending articles to a newsletter or internal digest.
    • Enrich a list of saved links with author, publication, date, engagement and topic metadata.
    • Track an author's or publication's output over time with scheduled runs.
