Brazilian CNPJ Bulk Lookup & Partner Records Scraper

Look up Brazilian companies by CNPJ in bulk, or discover companies by CNAE activity, state, city, legal nature or partner. Every result is an official-style Receita Federal registry record: legal name, status, CNAE codes, address, Simples/MEI flags, tax history, capital, and the full partner (QSA) list. Numeric and 2026 alphanumeric CNPJs are accepted, with no login, API key, or proxy.

What this actor does

  • Bulk lookup: paste a list, a spreadsheet column, or CSV text. CNPJs are normalized, check-digit validated, deduplicated, and resolved in one run.
  • Discovery: find companies by CNAE (primary or secondary), state, municipality code, legal nature, or a partner's masked CPF/CNPJ, then keep only the status you want.
  • Full partner records: name, masked document, person/company/foreign type, qualification, entry date, age band, country and legal representative.
  • Tax and status depth: Simples Nacional, MEI, option/exclusion dates, per-year tax regime, status reason, special status and registration dates.
  • Clean output: empty fields are omitted at every level; false and 0 are kept because they are real values.

Use cases

  • KYB and vendor onboarding: confirm a Brazilian counterparty's status, legal nature, capital and owners before signing.
  • Bulk CRM enrichment: turn a CNPJ column into industry, address, contact and ownership data.
  • Compliance screening: review status reason, Simples/MEI and partner structure for risk triage.
  • B2B prospecting: build lists by CNAE, state and municipality, then filter to active companies.
  • Ownership research: trace a masked partner across companies or review a company's QSA.
  • Market mapping: count companies in an activity or region before outreach.

FAQ

What is a CNPJ? Cadastro Nacional da Pessoa Jurídica — Brazil's 14-character company registration number issued by the Receita Federal. Since July 2026 new registrations can include letters (alphanumeric CNPJ), and this actor accepts both formats.

Where does the data come from? Public mirrors of the official Receita Federal CNPJ registry. The registry is published monthly, so very recent changes may not appear immediately.

Do I need an API key, login, cookie or proxy? No. The actor gets real data without credentials and without any proxy setup. Just fill in the input and run it.

What happens to invalid or unknown CNPJs? Invalid CNPJs are skipped before any request; unknown CNPJs are skipped by the source. The run summary reports invalid, not-found, duplicate and unavailable counts. No made-up records are ever added.

Why is the partner list (socios) empty for some companies? MEI and individual-entrepreneur registrations normally have no QSA. The registry can also withhold a QSA in specific legal cases. The field is simply omitted rather than filled with placeholders.

Why is email often missing? The public CNPJ registry rarely publishes company email addresses. When the source has one, it is included; otherwise the field is omitted. Phone and address data are more consistently available.

Does socios include equity percentages? No. The public registry exposes the partner list, qualification, entry date, age band and masked document, but not each partner's percentage. Percentages are only in the company's contract at the Junta Comercial.

Can I search by a partner's CPF? Yes, using the masked format shown in the QSA (for example ***123456**). Add a state or CNAE because the same six visible digits can match more than one person.

Is this affiliated with the Receita Federal or the Brazilian government? No. This is an independent, third-party actor that reads public registry mirrors and is not operated by or affiliated with any government agency.
