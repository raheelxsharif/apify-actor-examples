    TradeIndia B2B Product & Supplier Search Scraper is an Apify Actor published by Dataspryva. It accepts 4 input fields, and mode and keyword are required. Each result can carry up to 6 declared fields, including title, supplier, supplierCity, price, minOrder and productUrl. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    TradeIndia.com lists millions of products and manufacturer/exporter profiles across India's industrial, textile, chemical, food and engineering sectors. This actor runs a keyword search against TradeIndia and returns either the matching product listings or the matching supplier/company profiles as structured records, so you don't have to click through search result pages by hand.

    What this actor does

    • Searches TradeIndia by free-text keyword in one of two modes: product listings or supplier/company listings
    • Optionally narrows results to a single Indian city
    • Pulls price/price-range, minimum order quantity and product image for each product result
    • Pulls company name, city, product categories and phone number for each supplier result
    • Empty fields are omitted from records — a record never contains a null or empty value

    Use cases

    • Import/export sourcing — shortlist Indian manufacturers and exporters for a product category before reaching out
    • Price benchmarking — compare listed prices and MOQs for the same product across multiple sellers
    • B2B lead generation — build contact lists of suppliers filtered to a specific city or region
    • Market research — gauge how many sellers and what price spread exists for a given keyword on India's largest B2B marketplace
    • Procurement shortlisting — narrow a long supplier list down to companies based in a target city

    • productId — identifier parsed from the product's TradeIndia URL

    Questions

    What does this actor search?

    It runs the same keyword search box available on TradeIndia.com, in either product or supplier mode, and parses the result pages into structured records.

    Is a TradeIndia account or login required?

    No. Only the publicly visible search results are read; no login, cookies or API key are needed.

    Why do some records have no price or phone?

    TradeIndia sellers choose what to publish on their listing. If a seller didn't list a price, MOQ or phone number, that field is left out of the record rather than returned empty.

    Can I search for suppliers in a specific city without a product keyword?

    A keyword is required, but you can pair it with the city filter to narrow results — for example searching "cotton fabric" with city set to "Mumbai" only returns suppliers based there.

    How many results can I get per run?

    Up to the maxItems value you set, capped at 500. The actor stops early if TradeIndia's search runs out of further result pages for the keyword.

    Is this affiliated with TradeIndia?

    No. This is an independent, third-party actor that reads publicly available TradeIndia search results; it is not operated by or affiliated with TradeIndia.com.

    Why do city filter results sometimes come back empty?

    The city filter matches against the city text shown on each listing, so uncommon spellings or suppliers that didn't list a city won't match — try a broader keyword or remove the city filter.
