    ProductReview.com.au Company Ratings & Reviews Search is an Apify Actor published by Dataspryva. It accepts 14 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 11 declared fields, including recordType, companyName, companySlug, overallRating, reviewCount and category. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    The full README, with every mode, example and answer the publisher documents, is on the Apify Store page. Its sections: “What you can do”, “Output”, “Input examples”, “Input reference”, “Use cases” and “FAQ”.

    What it costs

    ProductReview.com.au Company Ratings & Reviews Search uses pay-per-event pricing: a fee for each result saved to the dataset, plus a start fee per run. Paid Apify plans pay less per result. The start fee is charged once per GB of run memory (at least once); the default run uses 4 GB, so a run starts with 4 start events, as the run totals include. You also pay Apify platform usage (compute and proxy) for the run, which these totals do not include.

    Input reference

    All 14 fields of the published input schema, in the publisher's order. Required fields are marked. Download the input schema as JSON.

    Input fields in the published schema
    FieldTypeDescription
    modeModestring requiredSearch for companies, or collect the reviews of specific companies.Default "searchCompanies"; Allowed searchCompanies, getReviews, search, reviews
    querySearch querystringKeyword to search for, such as 'travel insurance' or 'online electronics'. Used in mode=searchCompanies.Example "insurance"
    categoryCategorystringOptional category shortcut for mode=searchCompanies. It is combined with the query, or used alone when the query is empty.Default ""; Allowed , insurance, car-insurance, home-and-contents-insurance, health-insurance, life-insurance, travel-insurance, pet-insurance and 15 more
    companySlugsCompany slugs or URLsarrayCompanies to collect reviews for (mode=getReviews). Accepts ProductReview.com.au listing URLs or slugs, e.g. 'amazon' or 'https://www.productreview.com.au/listings/nrma-car-insurance'.Default []
    minRatingMinimum star ratingintegerOnly include reviews with at least this many stars (1-5).Default 1; Allowed 1 to 5
    maxRatingMaximum star ratingintegerOnly include reviews with at most this many stars (1-5).Default 5; Allowed 1 to 5
    reviewSortReview sort orderstringOrder of reviews in mode=getReviews.Default "overview"; Allowed overview, newest, ratingHighest, ratingLowest, mostLiked
    searchSortCompany sort orderstringOrder of company results in mode=searchCompanies.Default "relevance"; Allowed relevance, rating, numberOfReviews, popularity, lowestPrice, highestPrice
    verifiedOnlyVerified reviews onlybooleanOnly include reviews from verified reviewers in mode=getReviews.Default false
    withAttachmentsOnlyReviews with photos onlybooleanOnly include reviews that have at least one attached photo in mode=getReviews.Default false
    reviewKeywordReview keywordstringOnly include reviews whose text matches this keyword in mode=getReviews.
    dateFromReviews published fromstringEarliest review publication date (YYYY-MM-DD) in mode=getReviews.
    dateToReviews published untilstringLatest review publication date (YYYY-MM-DD) in mode=getReviews.
