    Instagram Posts & Reels Data Scraper is an Apify Actor published by Dataspryva. It accepts 2 input fields, and post_urls is required. Each result can carry up to 25 declared fields, including status, post_url, username, author_meta, media_type and like_count. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    The full README, with every mode, example and answer the publisher documents, is on the Apify Store page. Its sections: “What this actor does”, “Output per post”, “Status values”, “Input”, “Example output” and “Use cases”.

    What you get

    The Actor saves one record per result to an Apify dataset. Its build declares 25 fields in 2 dataset views. Fields without a value can be left out of a record.

    What it costs

    Instagram Posts & Reels Data Scraper uses pay-per-event pricing: a fee for each result saved to the dataset, plus a start fee per run. Paid Apify plans pay less per result. The start fee is charged once per GB of run memory (at least once); the default run uses 4 GB, so a run starts with 4 start events, as the run totals include. You also pay Apify platform usage (compute and proxy) for the run, which these totals do not include.

    Input reference

    All 2 fields of the published input schema, in the publisher's order. Required fields are marked. Download the input schema as JSON.

    Input fields in the published schema
    FieldTypeDescription
    post_urlsPost URLsarray requiredInstagram post, reel or IGTV URLs to scrape. Accepts /p/, /reel/, /reels/, /tv/, and canonical username/p/shortcode formats. Duplicates are silently de-duplicated.Example ["https://www.instagram.com/p/Dew5IIlNfMW/"]
    cookiesInstagram Cookies (Optional)stringOptional. Leave empty to scrape anonymously. Paste your own Instagram cookies in JSON format to also retry age-restricted and login-protected posts with your account; posts your account still cannot open keep their status, and a private post opens only if your account follows that account. It is a secret input, so the Apify Console hides and encrypts it. Format: [{"name":"sessionid","value":"...","domain":".instagram.com"}, ...]
