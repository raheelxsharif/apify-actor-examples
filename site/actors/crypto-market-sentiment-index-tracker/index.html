    Crypto Market Sentiment Index Tracker is an Apify Actor published by Dataspryva. It accepts 5 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 5 declared fields, including date, value, valueClassification, timestamp and datetimeUtc. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Scrape the Crypto Fear & Greed Index — the market-sentiment gauge that scores the whole crypto market from 0 (Extreme Fear) to 100 (Extreme Greed). Get the live reading, the last N daily values, or any historical date range, each with a numeric score, a sentiment classification, a unix timestamp, and clean ISO dates. Powered by the public alternative.me API. No API key, no login, no proxy.

    What this actor does

    • Three modes: latest, historical, dateRange
    • Live sentiment: the current index value plus seconds until the next update
    • Full history: every daily reading since the index began (2018+)
    • Date-range slicing: pull just the readings between two dates
    • Classification filter: keep only Extreme Fear, Fear, Neutral, Greed, or Extreme Greed readings
    • Clean, typed output: strings from the API are converted to real numbers and ISO dates
    • Empty fields are omitted — every field you see is populated

    Use cases

    • Trading signals — feed sentiment into contrarian buy/sell strategies
    • Dashboards & bots — display the live index in Telegram/Discord/Slack
    • Backtesting — align historical sentiment against price data
    • Research — study how fear and greed track market cycles
    • Alerts — trigger a notification when the market flips into Extreme Fear or Extreme Greed
    • Content — power newsletters and widgets with a fresh daily reading

    Questions

    What is the Crypto Fear & Greed Index?

    A daily 0–100 score of overall crypto-market sentiment, published by alternative.me. Low values mean investors are fearful (often seen as a buying opportunity); high values mean the market may be greedy and due for a correction.

    How are the classifications defined?

    Each score maps to a band: 0–25 Extreme Fear, 26–46 Fear, 47–54 Neutral, 55–75 Greed, 76–100 Extreme Greed.

    How often does it update?

    Once per day. The latest reading also includes timeUntilUpdate, the number of seconds until the next refresh.

    How far back does the history go?

    To the index's launch in early 2018. historical and dateRange can reach the entire archive.

    Do I need an API key or proxy?

    No. The data source is a free public API — this actor uses no authentication, cookies, or proxy.

    What happens with a bad date range?

    If a date is unparseable, or startDate is after endDate, the actor emits a single typed error record explaining the problem instead of failing silently.

    Why do some fields not appear?

    The actor omits any empty field. For example, timeUntilUpdate only appears on the current reading, never on historical rows.

    Is the index the same as the stock-market Fear & Greed Index?

    No — this is the crypto-specific index from alternative.me, built from volatility, momentum, volume, social media, dominance, and trends across the crypto market.
