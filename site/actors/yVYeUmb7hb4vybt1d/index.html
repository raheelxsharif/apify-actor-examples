Actors/TikTok Hashtag Momentum & Top Creator Tracker

TikTok Hashtag Momentum & Top Creator Tracker

Reads TikTok Creative Center's trending-hashtag list for any country and returns rank, post and view counts, a day-by-day momentum curve, and top creators behind each trend.

Open on ApifyGitHub source

Input schema

Input fields, types, required flags, descriptions, defaults, and prefills come from the published definition. Prefill examples are distinct from defaults.

FieldTypeRequiredDescription
modestringoptionalChoose 'Trending hashtags' for TikTok's ranked hashtag charts (post/view counts, popularity curve, creators) or 'Trending videos' for TikTok's ranked video charts (views, engagement, author stats).
countriesarrayoptional(mode=trendingHashtags) One or more markets to read the hashtag chart for. Each market adds its own ranked rows.
periodsarrayoptional(mode=trendingHashtags) Lookback windows in days. 7 shows recent spikes, 90 shows longer-term trends. Select one or more.

FieldTypeRequiredDescription
modestringoptionalChoose 'Trending hashtags' for TikTok's ranked hashtag charts (post/view counts, popularity curve, creators) or 'Trending videos' for TikTok's ranked video charts (views, engagement, author stats).
countriesarrayoptional(mode=trendingHashtags) One or more markets to read the hashtag chart for. Each market adds its own ranked rows.
periodsarrayoptional(mode=trendingHashtags) Lookback windows in days. 7 shows recent spikes, 90 shows longer-term trends. Select one or more.
includeOverallRankingbooleanoptional(mode=trendingHashtags) Include each market's overall top-3 hashtag chart. Turn off to read only the industry charts selected below.
scanAllIndustriesbooleanoptional(mode=trendingHashtags) Read all 19 industry charts for every market and window. This widens coverage from 3 to up to 60 hashtags per market and window, because each industry chart is read separately.
industriesarrayoptional(mode=trendingHashtags) Specific industry charts to read when 'Scan all industries' is off. Ignored when scanning all industries. 19 public categories are available.
includeCreatorLeadersbooleanoptional(mode=trendingHashtags) Add creator rows that aggregate the creators TikTok credits across the collected hashtags, sorted by how many trends they appear in.
minHashtagAppearancesintegeroptional(mode=trendingHashtags, creator leaderboard only) Only list creators credited on at least this many collected hashtag rows.
videoCountriesarrayoptional(mode=trendingVideos) Markets with a public TikTok trending-video chart. Select one or more.
videoPeriodsarrayoptional(mode=trendingVideos) Windows in days for the trending-video chart: 7 for videos created in the last week, 30 for the last month.
videoSortBystringoptional(mode=trendingVideos) Metric used for the video ranking.
videoOrganicOnlybooleanoptional(mode=trendingVideos) Rank only organic (non-promoted) videos. TikTok returns a different ranking for organic-only charts.
videoContentTagsarrayoptional(mode=trendingVideos) Query TikTok's video ranking by content tag and dedupe the videos. Each selected tag is a separate ranking read; 'All tags' also includes the unfiltered chart, returning up to ~24 videos per market, window and sort instead of 4. Leave empty for the current unfiltered ranking.
weeksBackintegeroptional(mode=trendingVideos) Add past weekly rankings by moving the chart end date back 7 days per step (0-4). Useful for comparing this week with earlier weeks.
includeTopCommentsbooleanoptional(mode=trendingVideos) Add each video's top comments and comment keywords. Each video's comments are read separately (up to 100 videos per run).
maxItemsintegeroptionalMaximum number of data rows to write per run. Creator rows are not counted.

TikTok Hashtag Momentum & Top Creator Tracker

Read TikTok's own ranked trending-hashtag and trending-video charts from the public Creative Center. For every market you choose, this actor returns exact post and view counts, a day-by-day popularity curve, a plain-language momentum read (rising, peaking, fading), the creators TikTok credits for each trend, and — when you ask for videos — the engagement metrics behind what is winning right now. No TikTok login, cookies or proxy setup.

What this actor does

  • Two collections: trendingHashtags reads TikTok's ranked hashtag charts; trendingVideos reads its ranked trending-video charts.
  • 27 hashtag markets, 3 windows: pick any mix of 27 markets and 7/30/90-day windows. The overall chart plus all 19 industry charts are available, widening one market/window from 3 hashtags to up to 60.
  • Momentum, not just a snapshot: every hashtag carries TikTok's daily popularity curve plus a derived momentum score, trend direction, trend stage, peak date and volatility.
  • Creator leaders: every trending hashtag includes TikTok's top-3 credited creators; an optional leaderboard aggregates them across the whole run so you can see who keeps appearing.
  • Trending videos: five markets with public video charts, ranked by views, engagement rate or 6-second view-through rate, with author followers, lifetime metrics and optional top comments. Content-tag rankings (Beauty & Care, Fashion, Food & Beverage, Lifestyle & Leisure, Technology & Finance) can be queried individually or all at once, deduping videos for up to ~24 per market/window/sort instead of 4.
  • Honest caps: TikTok's public charts show 3 hashtags per ranking and 4 videos per video ranking to guests. The actor explains its coverage in the run status instead of implying more.
  • Empty fields are omitted — never returned as null, "" or [].

Output per hashtag (rowType: "hashtag")

  • hashtagId, hashtagName, hashtagUrl — identity and the durable tag page link
  • rankIndex, isOverallRanking, rankingSlices — rank, whether it made the overall chart, and which industry charts it also ranked in
  • publishCnt — posts published with the hashtag during the window
  • vv — total video views those posts earned during the window
  • avgViewsPerPost — views divided by posts
  • popularityCurve — daily {date, value} points from TikTok's normalized 0–100 index
  • momentumChangePct, trendDirection, trendStage, peakDate, peakValue, curveVolatility — derived momentum signals
  • industryIds, industryNames — TikTok's category tags for the hashtag
  • topCreators[] — TikTok's credited creators (rank, ttuid, handleName, nickname, followerCount, countryCode, avatarUrl)
  • observedCountry, observedPeriod, scrapedAt, dataSource

Output per creator (rowType: "creator", optional)

  • creatorId, handleName, nickname, followerCount, countryCode, avatarUrl
  • appearances — how many collected hashtag rows credited this creator
  • hashtags[], bestRank, observedCountries[], observedPeriods[], scrapedAt

Output per video (rowType: "video")

  • videoId, videoUrl, embedUrl, caption, hashtags[]
  • createTime, createdAt, ageDays, coverUrl
  • author — id, username, displayName, bio, followerCount, avatarUrl
  • views, organicViews, engagementRate, sixSecondViewRate — window metrics
  • viewsLifeTime, organicViewsLifeTime, engagementRateLifeTime, sixSecondViewRateLifeTime — lifetime metrics
  • viewsPerDay, lifetimeViewsShare — derived velocity
  • topComments[], commentKeywords[] — only with Include top comments
  • contentTag — the content-tag ranking that surfaced this video (omitted for the unfiltered ranking)
  • rankingKey — provenance key: market:window:sort:weeksBack:tag (all = unfiltered)
  • observedCountry, observedPeriod, videoSortBy, weeksBack, scrapedAt

Use cases

  • Content calendars — slot a market's current hashtags, with trend stage, into next week's posting plan.
  • Trend timing — separate hashtags that are still breaking from ones that already peaked.
  • Creator partnerships — see which creators TikTok credits across several rising trends before reaching out.
  • Market comparison — run the same window across markets to find local trends rather than global noise.
  • Brand and campaign monitoring — schedule a daily run and watch your tag enter, climb or leave a market's chart.
  • Video performance research — study the engagement rate and 6-second view-through rate of videos TikTok is surfacing right now.

FAQ

Why does one market return only a few hashtags? TikTok's public Creative Center charts show the top 3 hashtags per ranking to visitors who are not signed in. This actor reads the overall chart plus the 19 industry charts, so a market and window yields up to 60 hashtags in total. There is no public path to a deeper ranking without a TikTok account.

What does the momentum score mean? momentumChangePct compares the average of the most recent third of the popularity curve with the average of the third before it. It is a percentage of TikTok's normalized 0–100 index, not raw views. Values of +10% or more are labelled rising, -10% or less falling.

Why are video charts limited to five markets? TikTok publishes trending-video charts publicly for the United States, Japan, Vietnam, Thailand and Indonesia. Hashtag charts cover 27 markets. The input lists exactly the markets that return video data.

How many videos can one ranking return? The public video chart shows 4 videos per market, window and sort. Adding extra weekly windows or selecting several markets or windows widens coverage; rows are deduplicated by video.

Why are comments optional? Top comments are read separately for each video, so they are opt-in and capped at 100 videos per run.

Are the image links permanent? coverUrl and creator avatarUrl are signed TikTok CDN links that expire after about 48 hours. The durable links are videoUrl, embedUrl and hashtagUrl, which always resolve.

Why do some hashtags have no industry? TikTok assigns industry categories to only some hashtags. When it publishes none, industryIds and industryNames are omitted for that row.

Does this actor need cookies or a proxy? No. It reads TikTok's public Creative Center data over plain HTTP. There is nothing to log in to and no proxy to configure.

How fresh is the data? The charts refresh continuously. For trend tracking, schedule one run per market per day; repeated runs at the same time return the same ranking.

Is this affiliated with TikTok? No. This is a third-party actor that reads publicly available Creative Center trend data; it is not affiliated with TikTok or ByteDance.
