    RentCafe City Apartment Price & Amenity Finder is an Apify Actor published by Dataspryva. It accepts 15 input fields, none of them required at the top level. Each result can carry up to 7 declared fields, including name, fullAddress, minPrice, maxPrice, rating and telephone. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    RentCafe.com lists apartment communities across the US with per-unit-type pricing, full amenity lists, star ratings, phone numbers and management-company contacts. This actor searches a city — or a set of saved RentCafe search URLs — and returns each community as a structured record for rent comparison, market research or a property-management lead list.

    What this actor does

    • Searches any US city RentCafe recognises by its URL slug, or accepts full RentCafe city search URLs
    • Uses RentCafe's own server-side filters: bedrooms, bathrooms, rent range, minimum rating, pet policy, amenity, property type, rent specials and move-in date
    • Orders results with RentCafe's own sort: best match, price low/high, rating high, newest, or 3D-tour listings
    • Paginates until your maxItems is reached or RentCafe's public result list ends
    • Merges the visible result card with RentCafe's schema.org JSON-LD so each record carries both per-unit prices and the full amenity/contact data
    • Omits empty fields — a field appears only when RentCafe published real data for that community

    Use cases

    • Rent comparison — pull every community in a city for a bedroom count and compare starting prices side by side
    • Lease shopping — narrow by rent range, pet policy, pool, garage or fitness center before you start calling
    • Property-management leads — collect community names, phone numbers and management companies across a city
    • Market research — track community-level minPrice/maxPrice and amenity coverage over time
    • Saved-search automation — paste an existing RentCafe search URL and turn it into a daily dataset

    Questions

    How do I find the city slug?

    The path segment right after /apartments-for-rent/ on RentCafe.com is the slug (e.g. rentcafe.com/apartments-for-rent/austin-tx/ → austin-tx). You can also type City, ST or paste the full search URL.

    Can I combine filters?

    Yes. The new filters use RentCafe's own server-side parameters and combine with each other. The old single path-segment filters still work for saved tasks, but the filter fields in the input form are the supported way to stack several conditions.

    Do I need a login or proxy?

    No. The listing pages are public and the actor needs no account, API key or proxy setup. It picks the network route it needs automatically.

    Why are there fewer records than the "Rentals Available" number?

    RentCafe's public city search paginates communities (usually 25 per page), while its rental count includes individual units/floor plans. The actor returns communities and tells you in the status message when the public result list is exhausted.

    Why does a listing URL return 403 to curl?

    RentCafe blocks some non-browser requests to property pages. The URLs are valid and open normally in a browser; photos and the dataset fields are unaffected.

    Why do some communities have no price or rating?

    RentCafe only shows what each property manager supplied. A community can be listed with "contact for pricing" and no reviews yet; those fields are omitted rather than filled with a guess.

    Is this affiliated with RentCafe?

    No. This is an independent actor that reads RentCafe's publicly available listing pages; it is not operated by or affiliated with RentCafe/Yardi.
