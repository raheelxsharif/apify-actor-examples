Each result can carry up to 6 declared fields, including name, fullAddress, minPrice, maxPrice, rating and url. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    RentCafe.com lists apartment communities across the US with per-unit-type pricing, amenities and management company information. This actor searches RentCafe for a city, with an optional bedroom-count, price-tier or amenity filter, and returns each apartment community as a structured record ready for a rent-comparison spreadsheet or a lead list.

    What this actor does

    • Searches apartment listings for any US city on RentCafe, identified by its location slug
    • Applies one RentCafe site filter at a time — bedroom count, price tier (e.g. under $1,000) or an amenity like pet-friendly, pool or garage
    • Refines results further on the client side by minimum rating, a price range, and a pet-friendly-only toggle, stacked on top of the site filter
    • Paginates automatically up to your requested item count
    • Empty fields are omitted from records — a field only appears when RentCafe actually published that data for the property

    Use cases

    • Rent comparison — pull every listing in a city for a bedroom count and compare starting prices side by side
    • Lease shopping — filter by amenity (pool, garage, pet-friendly) to shortlist communities that fit a specific need
    • Property management leads — collect managementCompany across a city to build a contact list
    • Market research — track minPrice/maxPrice across a city's listings over time to watch rent trends
    • Pet owner search — combine the pet-friendly filter with petFriendlyOnly for a narrower, double-checked result set

    Questions

    How do I find the location slug for my city?

    The URL path segment right after /apartments-for-rent/ on RentCafe.com is the slug to use (e.g. rentcafe.com/apartments-for-rent/austin-tx/ → austin-tx). You can also just type City, ST and the actor will normalize it, though results depend on RentCafe recognizing the generated slug.

    Can I combine two site filters, like 2-bedroom and pet-friendly?

    RentCafe's search only supports one filter segment per page. Pick the axis that matters most for filter, then use minPrice/maxPrice/minRating/petFriendlyOnly for additional narrowing on top of it.

    Do I need a login or proxy?

    No — RentCafe's listing pages are publicly accessible, and this actor needs no account, API key or proxy.

    Why do some listings have fewer fields than others?

    RentCafe only shows what each property manager provided — not every listing has a star rating or amenity list yet. Fields with no real data on the listing are omitted rather than returned empty.

    Why does RentCafe cap results around 500 per location?

    That's a limit of RentCafe's own search results, not of this actor; even for the largest cities, a single location/filter combination rarely surfaces more than ~500 listings.

    Is this affiliated with RentCafe?

    No. This is an independent, third-party actor that reads RentCafe's publicly available listing pages; it is not operated by or affiliated with RentCafe/Yardi.
