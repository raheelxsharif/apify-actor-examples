    Zalando New Arrivals, Sale & Size Finder is an Apify Actor published by Dataspryva. It accepts 8 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 8 declared fields, including name, brand, price, originalPrice, discountPercent and sizes. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Zalando is one of Europe's largest fashion platforms, selling clothing, shoes and accessories across 14 country-specific markets. This actor searches Zalando by keyword, or browses new arrivals or sale items, and returns price, discount, brand, color and available sizes for each product.

    What this actor does

    • Three working modes: search (keyword search), newArrivals (sorted by activation date), saleItems (sorted by biggest discount)
    • 14 European markets — Germany, UK, France, Netherlands, Italy, Spain, Poland, Belgium, Austria, Switzerland, Sweden, Denmark, Finland, Norway
    • Gender filter — men / women / kids / unisex
    • Sort control — popularity, new arrivals, price ascending/descending, or biggest discount
    • Empty fields are omitted — a field only appears when Zalando published that value for the product

    Use cases

    • Price monitoring — track a keyword's price spread and discount depth over time in one market
    • New-arrival tracking — watch what's freshly listed in a category or for a gender segment
    • Cross-market comparison — run the same keyword across several country values to compare pricing
    • Sale-season research — pull saleItems sorted by biggest discount during promotional periods
    • Size-availability checks — confirm which sizes are currently listed for a product line

    Questions

    Which countries are supported?

    14 European Zalando markets: Germany, UK, France, Netherlands, Italy, Spain, Poland, Belgium, Austria, Switzerland, Sweden, Denmark, Finland, and Norway.

    Why is category listed as an input but doesn't seem to filter results?

    It's accepted by the input form but isn't currently wired into a result filter — results reflect your keyword, gender, country, and sortBy only. Use a category-specific keyword (e.g. running shoes instead of just browsing a category) for similar narrowing.

    Why do I sometimes get fewer than maxItems results?

    Zalando paginates in fixed-size batches; a run stops once a page returns fewer items than requested, which can happen before maxItems is reached for narrow keywords or small markets.

    Why is originalPrice sometimes missing?

    It's only included when the item is actually discounted; full-price items only carry price.

    Do I need an account or cookies?

    No. All data comes from Zalando's public catalog pages for the selected market.

    Is this affiliated with Zalando?

    No, this is an independent third-party actor that reads Zalando's public catalog. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Zalando SE.
