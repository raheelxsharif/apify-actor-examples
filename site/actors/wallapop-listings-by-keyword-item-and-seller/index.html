    Wallapop Listings by Keyword, Item & Seller is an Apify Actor published by Dataspryva. It accepts 21 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 9 declared fields, including title, price, currency, condition, city and country. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Scrape Wallapop — the large second-hand marketplace in Spain, Italy and Portugal. Search by keyword or category, look up an item by its URL or ID, pull every active listing from a seller, or read a seller's profile, ratings and reviews. The actor returns prices, condition, photos, location, shipping, engagement counts and seller reputation as clean JSON. No login, no cookies, no API key, and no proxy setup.

    What this actor does

    • Four modes: search, itemDetails, sellerListings, sellerProfile.
    • Search by keyword or category: multiple keywords, all 21 Wallapop categories and 99 subcategories, with price, condition, posting-date, shipping, distance and sort filters.
    • Market-aware: Spain, Italy, Portugal, France, Germany and the United Kingdom by coordinates, with EUR prices and country-local listings.
    • Item lookup by URL or ID: full item detail including condition, attributes, images, reserves/sold status, views, favourites and conversations.
    • Seller tools: every active listing from one or more sellers, plus profile stats, rating breakdown, public contact details, business legal data and recent reviews.
    • Empty fields are omitted: a missing value is never returned as null, "" or [].

    Use cases

    • Price research: compare asking prices for the same product across Spanish, Italian and Portuguese cities.
    • Resale sourcing: follow a category with a price cap and spot under-priced listings early.
    • Seller vetting: read seller ratings, review history and public business details before buying.
    • Catalogue monitoring: export a shop's full active inventory to a spreadsheet on a schedule.
    • Demand analysis: rank listings by views, favourites and conversations.
    • Marketplace analytics: build a clean dataset of condition, attributes and geography.

    Output per listing

    Every listing record has recordType: "listing".

    • itemId, title, description, itemUrl, webSlug

    • imageUrl, imageUrls
    • city, country, postalCode, region, latitude, longitude, locationApproximate
    • distanceM — distance from the search centre, in metres (search mode)
    • publishedDate, modifiedDate — legacy Unix timestamps, when Wallapop provides them
    • publishedAt, modifiedAt — ISO 8601 versions
    • reserved, sold, expired, onHold, bumped, bumpType
    • isRefurbished, hasWarranty
    • shippable, shippingAllowed
    • views, favorites, conversations
    • seller, sellerId, sellerRating, sellerReviewCount, sellerVerified, sellerType, sellerIsTopProfile, sellerProfileUrl, sellerRegisteredAt
    • sellerContact — public address, phone, website and opening hours (business/pro sellers only)
    • sellerLegal — public legal name, email, registration number and registry (business sellers only)
    • scrapedAt

    Which optional fields appear depends on the mode and on what Wallapop publishes:

    • search includes location, dates, reserve/shipping flags and taxonomy out of the box. Condition, attributes, views, favourites and conversations require Include item details. Seller name, rating and verification require Include seller details.
    • itemDetails includes condition, attributes, flags, views, favourites, conversations and seller reputation. publishedDate/publishedAt are not available for a single item.
    • sellerListings includes condition, attributes, shipping, refurbished/warranty and seller fields. Location, dates, views and favourites require Include item details; published date is not available in this mode.

    Questions

    Do I need a Wallapop account, cookies or an API key?

    No. The actor reads publicly accessible listing and seller data without authentication.

    Do I need to configure a proxy?

    No. The actor connects directly to Wallapop; no proxy configuration is required.

    Which countries are supported?

    Spain, Italy and Portugal are the primary markets. France, Germany and the United Kingdom also return local listings when you choose their country or supply coordinates; inventory there is much smaller.

    Why are some fields missing from a record?

    Wallapop omits data for some listings, and some fields are mode-specific. Empty fields are left out rather than returned as null. Turn on Include item details for condition, attributes, views and favourites.

    Why did I get fewer rows than maxItems?

    Wallapop exhausted the matching results, or the country filter removed cross-border listings. The run status reports the requested and returned counts honestly. The actor never fabricates rows.

    Can I get sold items?

    Only if you already have the item URL. Keyword and category search return active listings, and seller catalogues return active listings. itemDetails reports sold: true when Wallapop marks the item sold.

    How many reviews can I get?

    The actor fetches reviews newest-first in pages of 40 until maxReviews is reached or Wallapop has no older reviews. Set maxReviews up to 2,000. The run status reports a shortfall if the source stops earlier.

    How fresh is the data?

    Every run reads the live marketplace. Values reflect Wallapop at the moment of collection.

    Is this affiliated with Wallapop?

    No. This is an independent third-party actor using publicly accessible marketplace data.

    Limits and notes

    Limitations

    • publishedDate is available in keyword/category search only. Wallapop does not expose a creation date on item or seller endpoints, so those modes omit it.
    • Seller contact details and business legal data are returned only when the seller has made them public (business, shop or pro accounts).
    • Reviews are fetched newest-first in pages of 40 until maxReviews (default 20, maximum 2,000) or the source stops. Verified full collections of 411/411, 1,226/1,226 and 1,714/1,714 reviews for sellers of those sizes; the practical ceiling is the seller's public review count.
    • sellerListings returns active listings only. Sold items are not searchable; an item URL that is already sold still works through itemDetails.
    • Nearest uses Wallapop's proximity ranking; the source does not guarantee a strict distance order. Sort on distanceM if you need exact ordering.
    • categoryId is Wallapop's top-level taxonomy id, while categoryName is the leaf name and categoryPath is the full hierarchy. Names follow the source locale.
    • Italy and Portugal have smaller catalogues. For the largest local result set use Most relevant or Nearest, and a distance that matches the country.
