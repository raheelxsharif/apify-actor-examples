    eBay Motors Search & Listing Detail Extractor is an Apify Actor published by Dataspryva. It accepts 21 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 19 declared fields, including title, price, priceFormatted, condition, titleStatus and year. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    eBay Motors lists vehicles and parts from private sellers and dealers across the US. This actor runs a vehicle or parts search, or opens specific item pages you already have, and returns structured records with pricing, vehicle identity, title status, VIN, mileage, seller details, returns and images.

    What this actor does

    • Searches eBay Motors vehicle listings by keyword, vehicle category (cars & trucks, motorcycles, boats, RVs, ATVs, commercial trucks), condition, price range, model-year range, maximum mileage, transmission, fuel type, buying format, seller and ZIP-radius search
    • Searches parts & accessories by keyword, parts category, condition, price and buying format
    • Enriches specific item URLs, bare item IDs or saved search URLs with full listing detail instead of running a new search
    • Paginates at eBay's 240-row page size through up to 200 result pages, with cross-page deduplication
    • Omits empty fields from every record — a field appears only when the listing actually shows that data

    Modes

    Mode What it returns
    searchVehicles Vehicle search-result records
    searchParts Parts & accessories search-result records
    byListingUrls Full detail records for item IDs/URLs; search URLs are parsed as search-result pages

    Use cases

    • Vehicle shopping research — compare price, mileage, title status and VIN across every matching listing
    • Dealer inventory tracking — re-run a seller search or ZIP-radius search to see what changed
    • Parts sourcing — search a parts category and keyword to compare available stock and pricing
    • Auction monitoring — track bids, time remaining and watchers on listings you are bidding on
    • Listing enrichment — feed a list of item IDs or URLs into byListingUrls for full structured detail

    Questions

    What is the difference between the three modes?

    searchVehicles and searchParts run an eBay Motors search with your filters. byListingUrls skips search and opens exactly the item URLs or IDs you supply; search URLs passed there are parsed as search-result pages.

    Which filters actually work on eBay Motors?

    Price, condition, model year, transmission, fuel type, buying format, seller, ZIP radius and sort order are eBay's own server-side parameters. There is no reliable server-side maximum-mileage parameter, so maxMileage is applied by the actor after parsing — a tight cap may need more pages to fill maxItems. Listings with no printed mileage are kept rather than dropped blindly.

    Why is VIN or title status missing on some records?

    eBay prints VIN on most dealer detail pages but omits it for some private/classified listings. Title status appears on Motors cards and detail pages when the seller provides it. Both fields are omitted rather than guessed.

    What is in itemSpecifics?

    The detail page's full dt/dd specification table (VIN, engine, colours, warranty, country of origin, and any other rows eBay prints). Turn includeItemSpecifics off to keep records smaller.

    Why do I get isTrendingRecommendation: true records?

    That only happens in searchVehicles mode when you leave searchQuery empty and apply no vehicle filter — eBay shows a curated "Trending Vehicles" module instead of a real search page. Add a keyword or any filter for genuine matches.

    How many listings can one run return?

    Up to maxItems, capped at 10,000. Pages hold up to 240 cards and eBay caps every query at 200 pages; split by category, price or mileage for larger sweeps.

    Does this include sold or ended listings?

    No. Sold/completed results require an eBay sign-in, and this actor never asks for cookies or logins.

    Do I need a proxy or API key?

    No. The actor tries a direct request, then a public cookieless mirror, then an Apify proxy if needed, and chooses automatically. There are no credentials to configure.

    Is this affiliated with eBay?

    No. This is an independent, third-party actor that reads eBay Motors' public listing pages; it is not operated by or affiliated with eBay Inc.
