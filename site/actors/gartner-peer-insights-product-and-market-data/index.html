    Gartner Peer Insights Product & Market Data Extractor is an Apify Actor published by Dataspryva. It accepts 14 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 15 declared fields, including type, productName, vendorName, averageRating, totalReviews and marketName. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    The full README, with every mode, example and answer the publisher documents, is on the Apify Store page. Its sections: “Modes”, “Input”, “Output: product (type: "product")”, “Output: review (type: "review")”, “Output: market (type: "market")” and “Output: category (type: "category")”.

    What it costs

    Gartner Peer Insights Product & Market Data Extractor uses pay-per-event pricing: a fee for each result saved to the dataset, plus a start fee per run. Paid Apify plans pay less per result. The start fee is charged once per GB of run memory (at least once); the default run uses 4 GB, so a run starts with 4 start events, as the run totals include. You also pay Apify platform usage (compute and proxy) for the run, which these totals do not include.

    Input reference

    All 14 fields of the published input schema, in the publisher's order. Required fields are marked. Download the input schema as JSON.

    Input fields in the published schema
    FieldTypeDescription
    modeModestring requiredChoose what to collect: product profiles with reviews, a market's product listing, or the full market and category directory.Default "categories"; Allowed products, market, categories
    productUrlsProduct URLs or slugsarrayGartner Peer Insights product page URLs or product slugs, e.g. https://www.gartner.com/reviews/product/salesforce-sales-performance-management or salesforce-sales-performance-management. Used in products mode.Default []
    marketSeoNameMarketstringGartner market slug or full market URL, e.g. sales-force-automation-platforms or https://www.gartner.com/reviews/market/sales-force-automation-platforms. Used in market mode.Default ""
    includeProductDetailsFetch full product details in market modebooleanWhen on, market mode fetches each product profile and its reviews instead of writing the fast market listing records.Default false
    maxReviewsPerProductMax reviews per productintegerMaximum public reviews to collect for each product. Set 0 to collect only the product profile. Reviews are paged until this limit or the public review list ends.Default 50; Example 2; Allowed 0 to 1000
    useFiltersAdd star-rating passes for more reviewsbooleanAfter paging, query each star rating once to surface additional unique public reviews. Has no effect when a specific star rating is selected below.Default true
    reviewSortReview sort orderstringHow Gartner orders public reviews before paging.Default "most-helpful"; Allowed most-helpful, most-recent, least-recent
    reviewRatingFilter reviews by star ratingstringCollect only reviews with this star rating, or all ratings.Default "all"; Allowed all, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1
    reviewerTypeFilter reviews by reviewer typestringCollect reviews from all reviewers, end users, or vendor partners.Default "all"; Allowed all, end-user, partner
    lastTwelveMonthsOnly reviews from the last 12 monthsbooleanLimit reviews to those published in the last twelve months.Default false
    marketSortMarket product sort orderstringHow products are ordered inside a market listing.Default "ratingsCount_desc"; Allowed ratingsCount_desc, ratingsCount_asc, averageRating_desc, averageRating_asc, name_asc, name_desc
    marketCompanySizeFilter market products by company sizestringUse Gartner's company-size facet when listing products in a market.Default "all"; Allowed all, <50M USD, 50M-1B USD, 1B-10B USD, 10B+ USD, Gov't/PS/Ed
    maxItemsMax recordsintegerMaximum number of market, product, review, or category records to save.Default 100; Example 3; Allowed 1 to 10000
    proxyConfigurationProxy configurationobjectOptional. The actor works without a proxy and switches automatically only if Gartner blocks the request.
