    SpareRoom UK Flatshare Budget & Radius Search is an Apify Actor published by Dataspryva. It accepts 8 input fields, and mode is required. Each result can carry up to 7 declared fields, including title, price, priceType, city, postcode and propertyType. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    SpareRoom is the UK's largest site for rooms in shared houses, flats and studios. This actor runs a SpareRoom room search for a city with a budget range and a search radius from the centre, or fetches specific listing URLs directly, and turns every advert into a structured record: rent, bills-included status, neighbourhood, property type, landlord type, availability and photo.

    What this actor does

    • Searches rooms to rent in any UK city or area, with min/max monthly budget and a 0–50 mile radius from the city centre
    • Walks search result pages automatically until maxItems is reached or listings run out
    • Fetches full detail for a specific list of SpareRoom listing URLs instead of running a search
    • Surfaces landlord signals — advertiser role, profile photo, listing age and SpareRoom's verified-advertiser badge
    • Empty fields are omitted from records — a field only appears when the advert actually shows that data

    Use cases

    • Flat-hunting tools — surface rooms within budget and commute radius of a target address
    • Rental market tracking — monitor asking rents by city and neighbourhood over successive runs
    • Buy-to-let research — benchmark room rents in a target postcode before purchasing an investment property
    • Letting agency monitoring — watch how quickly a competitor's adverts turn over using daysOld
    • Flatshare market analysis — quantify how bills-included pricing and landlord type vary across a city

    Questions

    Is this affiliated with SpareRoom?

    No. This is an independent, third-party actor that reads SpareRoom's publicly visible search and listing pages; it is not operated by or affiliated with SpareRoom.co.uk.

    Do I need a SpareRoom account?

    No — only publicly visible listing data is collected. Contact details hidden behind SpareRoom's own login are not returned.

    Is price quoted per month or per week?

    Check the paired priceType field — advertisers quote both, and the actor reports the price exactly as shown rather than converting it. The minBudget/maxBudget inputs are still applied on a monthly basis by SpareRoom's own search.

    Does setting roomType actually filter the results?

    Not yet — the field is accepted by the input schema, but search results aren't currently narrowed by it server-side. Filter the output records on title or propertyType if you need to isolate a room type.

    Why do some listings have no postcode?

    Many advertisers only publish a neighbourhood name or a partial postcode to protect their privacy; when no postcode is shown, the field is left out rather than guessed.

    What does landlordType tell me?

    Whether the advert comes from a live-in landlord, a current tenant looking for a flatmate, or a letting agent — useful as a proxy for how the tenancy will be handled.

    What does a high daysOld value usually mean?

    It means the advert has been live a while without being taken down, which can indicate the room isn't renting easily — sometimes a sign there's room to negotiate on price.

    Can I search outside the UK?

    No — SpareRoom's UK site is the data source, so coverage spans England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland only.

    How fresh is the data?

    Everything is fetched live when the actor runs, so prices and availability reflect SpareRoom at that moment.
