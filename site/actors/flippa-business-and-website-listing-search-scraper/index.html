    Flippa Business & Website Listing Search Scraper is an Apify Actor published by Dataspryva. It accepts 13 input fields, none of them required at the top level. Each result can carry up to 37 declared fields, including title, property_type, price, revenue_per_month, profit_per_month and url. It costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on Apify's Free plan and $0.49 on the Business plan.

    What it does

    Flippa is the largest marketplace for buying and selling websites, ecommerce stores, SaaS products, mobile apps, domains and other online businesses. This actor queries Flippa's search catalog — either by pasting a Flippa search URL or by setting structured filters — and returns every matching listing as a clean record with financials, traffic, verification and seller data.

    What this actor does

    • Searches Flippa listings by pasting any flippa.com/search URL, or by setting structured filter fields (property type, status, price, revenue, profit, verification, keyword)
    • Covers every Flippa property type: websites, ecommerce stores, SaaS, iOS/Android apps, domains, newsletters, digital products, content sites and services
    • Returns financial metrics (price, revenue, profit, multiples), traffic metrics, and Flippa's own revenue/traffic verification flags for each listing
    • Sorts results by newest, price, revenue or profit, and paginates automatically up to the requested item count
    • Empty fields are omitted from records — a field only appears when Flippa actually published that data for the listing

    Use cases

    • Deal sourcing — scan open listings filtered by profit multiple and verified revenue to find under-priced acquisition targets
    • Niche monitoring — track the newsletter, content or digital-product categories for new deal flow
    • SaaS acquisition research — filter SaaS listings by monthly revenue and age to build a shortlist
    • App portfolio scouting — scan iOS and Android app listings with verified download counts
    • Market benchmarking — compare price-to-revenue and price-to-profit multiples across industries and property types

    Questions

    Do I need a Flippa account or API key?

    No — this actor reads Flippa's public search catalog; no login or credentials are required.

    Can I get sold listings?

    No. Flippa's public catalog does not expose sold listings, so only open, under_offer and closed (unsold, ended) listings are available — and in practice under_offer/closed currently return no records at all, only open does.

    How many listings can one run return?

    Up to maxItems, capped at 10,000. For very broad categories, narrow with price or revenue filters to stay under that cap.

    Why does price sometimes exceed the priceMax I set?

    The price filter is applied against the listing's asking/buy-it-now price at query time. For auctions, the current bid shown in price can rise above that as bidding progresses, while buy_it_now_price keeps the original asking price. Filter on saleMethod: "classified" if you need the returned price to always stay inside your range.

    Why does property_type not exactly match the category I filtered by?

    Flippa filters on a high-level category (e.g. newsletter) but the record's property_type reports the more specific platform Flippa detected (e.g. beehiiv). The filter still worked correctly — group by the filter you passed rather than the returned value if you need the broad category.

    Are digital_product and content property types useful?

    They're valid filter values, but at any given time Flippa may have zero active listings in those categories; the actor returns an empty dataset rather than an error when that happens.

    How fresh is the data?

    Every run queries Flippa live, so results reflect the marketplace at the moment the actor runs.

    Is this affiliated with Flippa?

    No. This is an independent, third-party actor that reads Flippa's public listing data; it is not operated by or affiliated with Flippa.com.
