    Instagram scrapers and data tools

    Dataspryva publishes 6 Apify Actors that read Instagram. Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Prices on the Free plan run from $0.49 to $3.00 per 1,000 results.

    Which Instagram Actor do you need?

    • Instagram Comments Scraper for Reels & Posts: Returns status, postUrl, postShortcode and 20 more fields. $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.
    • Instagram Downloader API - Reels, Video & Photo: Returns status, filename, post_url and 8 more fields. $0.49 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.
    • Instagram Posts & Reels Data Scraper: Returns Status, Post URL, Username and 22 more fields. $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.
    • Instagram Profile Scraper - Bio & Posts: Returns Username, Post URL, Type and 27 more fields. $0.70 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.
    • Instagram Search Scraper - Keywords & Hashtags: Returns Keyword, Post URL, Username and 23 more fields. $3.00 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.
    • Instagram Transcript Scraper - Reels & Video: Returns status, postUrl, shortCode and 19 more fields. $0.50 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.
