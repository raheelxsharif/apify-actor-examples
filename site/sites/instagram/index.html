    Instagram scrapers and data tools

    Dataspryva publishes 4 Apify Actors that read Instagram. Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Prices on the Free plan run from $0.49 to $0.99 per 1,000 results.

    Which Instagram Actor do you need?

    • Instagram Downloader API - Reels, Video & Photo: Download Instagram photos, videos, Reels and carousel posts from post links or usernames, up to 100 of each per run. Public posts and profiles need no login; add your own cookies for age-restricted posts and private accounts you follow. Each file gets a download link, size, resolution and length.
    • Instagram Posts & Reels Data Scraper: Scrape public Instagram posts, Reels, IGTV and carousels from a list of links, no login. Get caption, hashtags, author, photo and video links, likes, comments, video plays, location and music. Export to Excel, CSV or JSON.
    • Instagram Profile Scraper - Bio & Posts: Scrape public Instagram profiles from a list of usernames: bio, followers, following, post count, verified badge and bio links. Add recent posts with captions, hashtags, likes and comments. Export to Excel, CSV or JSON.
    • Instagram Transcript Scraper - Reels & Video: Instagram video to text: paste many reel, post or IGTV links and get each transcript with creator, caption and likes. Reads Instagram captions or uses AI speech-to-text in 29 languages, with optional timestamps per phrase. Export to JSON, CSV or Excel for subtitles and research.
