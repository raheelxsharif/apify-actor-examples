    Google Maps scrapers and data tools

    Dataspryva publishes 7 Apify Actors that read Google Maps. Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Each costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.

    Which Google Maps Actor do you need?

    • Google Maps Business Lead & Email Finder: Find business leads on Google Maps by category, keyword, or location and collect name, address, phone, website, rating, review count, and coordinates. Optionally adds emails and social links found on each business's own website.
    • Google Maps Grid-Based Market Saturation Scanner: Cover a bounding box, circle, or custom polygon with a search grid to collect every business Google Maps exposes for a query, well beyond the usual 120-result cap on a single search.
    • Google Maps Radius Search Around a Coordinate: Find Google Maps places within a set radius of an exact latitude/longitude, with rating, reviews, address, phone, website, coordinates, and distance from the center point.
    • Google Maps Related Place & Competitor Finder: Find the related and 'people also search for' places Google Maps shows on a business, hotel, or landmark page. Get each related place's name, rating, review count, category, coordinates, and image, linked back to the place you searched.
    • Google Maps Restaurant Menu & Popular Dish Extractor: Extract a restaurant's menu items from its Google Maps place page - dish name, photo, Popular badge, menu section, and an external menu link when the business has one.
    • Google Maps Timezone & Local Time Lookup: Resolve the IANA timezone, current local time, UTC offset, and daylight-saving information for any coordinate. Accepts single or batch lat/lng inputs.
    • Google Maps Top List & Ranked Places Extractor: Pull the ranked places from a Google Maps Top list query like 'Top hotels in Paris', or from a list/search URL, with optional full place-page enrichment.
