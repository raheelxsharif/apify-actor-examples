    TikTok scrapers and data tools

    Dataspryva publishes 2 Apify Actors that read TikTok. Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Each costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.

    Which TikTok Actor do you need?

    • TikTok Hashtag Momentum & Top Creator Tracker: Reads TikTok Creative Center's trending-hashtag list for any country and returns rank, post and view counts, a day-by-day momentum curve, and top creators behind each trend.
    • TikTok Keyword & Brand Mention Monitor: Searches TikTok for posts matching any keyword, brand name, or handle, flagging caption text matches and listing formally tagged mentions, engagement, and author details.
