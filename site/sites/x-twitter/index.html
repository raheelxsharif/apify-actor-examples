    X (Twitter) scrapers and data tools

    Dataspryva publishes 5 Apify Actors that read X (Twitter). Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Each costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.

    Which X (Twitter) Actor do you need?

    • Twitter (X) Media Downloader: Download videos, photos, GIFs, audio and poster images from public Twitter/X posts - no login, no cookies. Works with post links or usernames, supports every video quality, audio-only M4A/MP3, quoted-post media and public signed download links.
    • Twitter (X) Space Scraper: Collect public X Spaces without login. Get the Space title, state, schedule, live and replay listener counts, host and participants, replay audio with a downloadable file, and an optional captions/AI transcript.
    • X Article Announcement Post Metadata Collector: Turn an X (Twitter) Article's announcement post or article link into structured metadata: title, preview text, cover image, publish date, and the announcement post with its author.
    • X Broadcast Viewer History & Replay Link Scraper: Get a public X broadcast's title, state, start/end times, viewer counts over time, thumbnail, broadcaster profile, and an optional replay stream link, resolved from a broadcast link or ID.
    • X Community Activity & Member Finder: Collect public X Community posts, about info, rules and members with no login required. Get the community profile, member count, ranked posts with engagement metrics, authors and media.
