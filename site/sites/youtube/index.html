    YouTube scrapers and data tools

    Dataspryva publishes 6 Apify Actors that read YouTube. Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Each costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.

    Which YouTube Actor do you need?

    • YouTube Channel About, Playlists & Posts Finder: Look up a YouTube channel's About page details - subscribers, views, country, join date, links - with optional playlist and Community Post snapshots nested into the same row. No cookies or login required.
    • YouTube Community Tab Post & Poll Scraper: Read a YouTube channel's Community tab and get structured records for every text post, image, poll, and shared video, with vote counts and optional comment threads.
    • YouTube Hashtag Video & Shorts Ranking Scraper: Look up one or more YouTube hashtags and get the videos and Shorts currently ranked under them, in order, with view count, channel, duration and the hashtag's own total video and channel counts.
    • YouTube Live Stream & Replay Chat Extractor: Collect chat messages, Super Chats, Super Stickers, and membership events from an active YouTube livestream or an ended stream's saved chat replay, with author and timestamp detail.
    • YouTube Search Suggestions Scraper: Get public YouTube autocomplete suggestions for keyword research, content discovery, and SEO planning.
    • YouTube SponsorBlock Segments Scraper: Get community-reported SponsorBlock segments for any YouTube video: sponsor reads, intros, outros, self-promos, filler, highlights, and more, with start/end times and votes.
