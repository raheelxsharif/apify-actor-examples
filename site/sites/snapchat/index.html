    Snapchat scrapers and data tools

    Dataspryva publishes 5 Apify Actors that read Snapchat. Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Each costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.

    Which Snapchat Actor do you need?

    • Snapchat Highlight & Story Media Downloader: Pull individual snaps from a Snapchat profile's saved highlight albums and live story, with downloadable video/image links, post timestamps, and the creator's subscriber count and bio attached.
    • Snapchat Publisher & Show Episode Finder: Look up Snapchat publisher and Snap Show profiles by URL or ID: subscriber count, premium episodes and seasons, and publisher Spotlight content with media links and engagement stats.
    • Snapchat Snap Map Place & Live Snap Extractor: Search Snapchat's Snap Map by place name or landmark and get each place's coordinates, category, favorite and snap counts, plus the media link for its most recent public snap.
    • Snapchat Spotlight Comment & Reaction Scraper: Get comment threads from Snapchat Spotlight videos, by direct video URL or by creator username. Each comment includes text, commenter display name and Bitmoji IDs, reaction counts, posting time, and reply count.
    • Snapchat Topic Spotlight Feed & Content Tag Extractor: Reads a Snapchat topic's public Spotlight feed and returns each video with creator, engagement counts, AI-generated title/keywords, and related content-classification tags.
