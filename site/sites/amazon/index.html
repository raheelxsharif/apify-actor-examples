    Amazon scrapers and data tools

    Dataspryva publishes 5 Apify Actors that read Amazon. Each one answers a different question, so pick by the data you need. Each costs $0.99 per 1,000 results on the Free plan.

    Which Amazon Actor do you need?

    • Amazon Brand Store Page & Merchandising Crawler: Crawl an Amazon Brand Store's navigation, creative modules (images, video, CTAs), and every product link found on its pages. Resolves a brand's Store URL automatically from a product page or ASIN.
    • Amazon Deals Grid Tracker by Category & Discount: Pull Amazon's Today's Deals grid across 23 marketplaces, with deal price, list price, savings amount/percent, deal type, brand, and department for each product. Filter by category, brand, price range, and discount range.
    • Amazon Review Aggregates & Extended History Scraper: Collect Amazon customer reviews and rating summaries by product URL, ASIN, or keyword across 19 marketplaces, with star-rating and sort filters, reviewer images, and an optional session for deeper review history.
    • Amazon Search, Category & Seller Listings Extractor: Get Amazon products from search results, categories, seller storefronts, or ASIN lookups across 20+ marketplaces, with price, rating, badges, deals, and delivery details.
    • Amazon Wishlist & Registry Scraper: Scrape public/shareable Amazon wishlists, gift lists, baby registries, and wedding registries from URLs you supply. Get list metadata, item ASINs, prices, quantities needed/purchased, priority, and comments.
