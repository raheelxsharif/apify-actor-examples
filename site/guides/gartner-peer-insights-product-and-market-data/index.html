    Gartner Peer Insights Product & Market Data Extractor: CSV and Excel export

    Get Gartner Peer Insights product profiles with rating breakdowns, available customer reviews with reviewer industry/company size/job title, or a market's full product and category listing. No login required.

    Fix common problems

    Do I need a proxy?

    No. Direct requests are attempted first. If Gartner blocks the network path, the actor escalates automatically; you do not need to configure a proxy.

    Why is totalReviews larger than the number of review rows?

    totalReviews is Gartner's full rating count. Public review pages expose one review at a time per result page, so a run with maxReviewsPerProduct: 50 collects at most 50 public reviews unless additional star-rating passes find new ones.

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.
