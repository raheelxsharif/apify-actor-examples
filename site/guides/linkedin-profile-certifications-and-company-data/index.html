    LinkedIn Profile, Certifications & Company Data Extractor export

    Turn LinkedIn profile URLs into structured records with work history, education, certifications, honors, volunteer work, personal projects and current-employer company details, with no login required.

    This tutorial runs LinkedIn Profile, Certifications & Company Data Extractor through the Apify API and saves what it returns as a CSV file, an Excel workbook or JSON, keeping the 8 columns its publisher puts first: name, headline, location, profileUrl, followersCount, connectionsText, scrapedAt and summary.

    What people export it for

    • Recruiting and candidate research — review public certifications, education, languages, and project history before an outreach
    • Sales and CRM enrichment — append current role, employer, and company firmographics to a list of profile URLs
    • Credential and award research — collect public certifications, honors, publications, and patents across a population
    • Talent-market analysis — study the distribution of public skills, languages, and employers in a cohort
    • Profile monitoring — refresh a saved URL list and compare current employer, school, and public sections over time

    Fix common problems

    Why are some fields missing on a particular profile?

    LinkedIn either hides that field from logged-out visitors or the profile owner never filled it in. The record omits it instead of returning a placeholder.

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.
