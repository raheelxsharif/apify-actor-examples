    Amazon Deals Grid Tracker by Category & Discount: export to CSV, Excel or JSON

    Pull Amazon's Today's Deals grid across 23 marketplaces, with deal price, list price, savings amount/percent, deal type, brand, and department for each product. Filter by category, brand, price range, and discount range.

    • Deal feeds and newsletters — a fresh, structured snapshot of today's discounts by category and collection.
    • Competitive intelligence — which brands and categories Amazon is discounting, and how deep.

    • Shopping assistants — surface highly rated deals that meet a price and discount floor.
    • Market research — compare discount depth and category mix across Amazon marketplaces.

    Fix common problems

    Why do some rows have no dealPrice or discount?

    Amazon genuinely omits price and discount data for a subset of deals (for example, some coupon-only promotions), and it omits a local price when only a converted display price is available. The actor never guesses, converts or fills a zero. Price and discount filters exclude rows that cannot be verified.

    Why is percentClaimed missing on many rows?

    Amazon exposes claim progress mainly for limited/urgent offers such as Lightning Deals. It is populated when present and omitted otherwise.

    Why don't I see shipping, seller, stock, Best Sellers Rank or review text?

    Those fields are not part of the Deals grid or its product-summary data. This actor stays focused on deals; use a product-details or reviews actor for those fields.

    More from the publisher:

    • Why is the currency different from what I see on Amazon?
    • Why does a collection sometimes return a COLLECTION_NOT_FOUND status?
    • Why can sortBy take longer?
    • Why does sourceUrl return 503 in a plain script?

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.
