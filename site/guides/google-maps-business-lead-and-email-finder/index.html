    Google Maps Business Lead & Email Finder: export to CSV, Excel or JSON

    Find business leads on Google Maps by category, keyword, or location and collect name, address, phone, website, rating, review count, and coordinates. Optionally adds emails and social links found on each business's own website.

    This tutorial runs Google Maps Business Lead & Email Finder through the Apify API and saves what it returns as a CSV file, an Excel workbook or JSON, keeping the 8 columns its publisher puts first: recordType, name, googleBusinessCategories, street, city, state, zip and country.

    Fix common problems

    Do I need an API key, cookies or a proxy?

    No. Searches and business websites are read from public pages. You do not configure a connection.

    Why is a row missing an email?

    Not every business has a website, and not every website publishes an address. Some use a contact form or a booking widget. The row is still a valid lead unless you choose onlySaveResults: "onlyWithEmails"; that filter keeps the matching rows among the places fetched up to your lead cap.

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.
