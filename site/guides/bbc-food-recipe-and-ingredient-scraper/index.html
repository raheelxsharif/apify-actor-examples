    BBC Food Recipe & Ingredient Scraper: export to CSV, Excel or JSON

    Find BBC Food recipes by keyword or direct recipe URL and get the full ingredient list, step-by-step method, timings, servings, rating, and cuisine for each one.

    This tutorial runs BBC Food Recipe & Ingredient Scraper through the Apify API and saves what it returns as a CSV file, an Excel workbook or JSON, keeping the 6 columns its publisher puts first: title, author, cuisine, prepTime, cookTime and sourceUrl.

    Swap in any field from the output reference to export it too.

    What people export it for

    • Recipe app content — populate a cooking app with structured ingredients and steps instead of raw HTML
    • Meal planning tools — pull servings, timings, and calories to feed into a planner
    • Content research — gather a batch of recipes by cuisine for editorial or recipe-roundup work
    • Nutrition tracking — collect calorie figures across a set of dishes where BBC Food publishes them

    Fix common problems

    Why do some recipes have no calories or rating?

    Not every BBC Food recipe page publishes a nutrition panel or carries community ratings. Those fields are left off the record rather than filled with a placeholder.

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.
