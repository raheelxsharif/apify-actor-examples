    Florida Sunbiz Registry Lookup by Name, Agent & FEI: CSV and Excel export

    Look up Florida business entities on the Sunbiz registry by entity name, officer, registered agent, FEI/EIN, document number, ZIP, or street address, and get status, addresses, officers, annual filings, and filed-document links for each match.

    What people export it for

    • Due diligence / KYC — verify a Florida vendor's status, FEI/EIN, registered agent, and filing history
    • Ownership and conflict checks — map every entity tied to an officer or registered agent
    • Registered-agent monitoring — track the companies represented by an agent
    • Legal research — review amendments, mergers, reinstatements, and previous names by document number
    • Lead enrichment — add verified Florida business identity, address, and people data to a CRM
    • Trademark research — collect mark owners, classes, and first-use/expiration dates

    Fix common problems

    Why does a name search sometimes return entities that do not contain my term?

    Sunbiz name, officer/agent, FEI, ZIP, and address indexes are ordered lists. If your term has no exact match, the site returns the nearest entries in its index instead of zero results. Only documentNumber is a true exact lookup. Use the entityType filter to hide trademarks and unrelated filing types.

    Why are some records missing FEI/EIN, addresses, or a registered agent?

    Older, inactive, or certain filing types genuinely do not have those values on file. The actor reports exactly what Sunbiz publishes and omits empty fields instead of inventing placeholders.

    Why do trademarks have a different set of fields?

    Trademark filings do not have a registered agent, officers, or standard addresses. They publish owners, mark type/class, first-use dates, and an expiration date instead; those fields are included when present.
