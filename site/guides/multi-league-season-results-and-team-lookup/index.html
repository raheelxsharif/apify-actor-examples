    What people export it for

    • Season recaps and standings — pull every result of a season to build tables, summaries or content.
    • Odds and market research — export average, maximum and per-bookmaker 1X2 prices for a whole season.
    • Team reference data — collect stadium, city, country, coach and badge details for every team in a league.
    • Historical analysis — run once per season to compare results and odds across decades.
    • Data enrichment — join eventId, teamId and team URLs against datasets you maintain.

    Fix common problems

    Do I need a proxy, login or API key?

    No. The actor fetches public OddsPortal pages and picks its own network route automatically.

    Why did one league return no rows?

    OddsPortal sometimes answers a league-season page with "no odds available from your selected bookmakers" for anonymous visitors, and a few league-seasons are empty upstream on the main domain (ACB 2024-2025 is one). The actor retries through a fresh route, optionally tries the same site's regional edition (regionalFallback), validates that the served season is the one you asked for, and reports the exact reason in the status message instead of failing silently — it never returns another season's matches.

    Why does the bookmaker count vary between runs?

    OddsPortal shows the bookmakers available in the location of the request. Runs on US-based exits typically see fewer bookmakers than runs from other regions; bookmakerCount and the per-bookmaker list show exactly what a match had, and odds are the real prices from that set.

    More from the publisher:

    • Why is there no short code like "NEW" for teams?

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.
