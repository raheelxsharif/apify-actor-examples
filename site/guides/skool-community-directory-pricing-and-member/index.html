    Skool Community Directory, Pricing & Member Finder: CSV and Excel export

    Browse Skool's public community directory by category, pull pricing and member-count details for communities you already know, and list top members visible on a public community page.

    What people export it for

    • Niche research — compare member counts, pricing and ratings across every community in a category
    • Competitive tracking — follow a competitor's member growth, price changes and owner activity
    • Creator prospecting — find paid communities and their owners in a specific topic

    • Member research — collect the publicly visible members, admins and online users of a community

    Fix common problems

    Do I need a Skool account, cookies or a proxy?

    No. Every mode runs without a login, cookies or proxy setup.

    Why are category and categorySlug sometimes missing?

    A global directory search does not label each row with a category. Those fields are filled when you browse a category or when a large request is partitioned by category.

    Why does getMembers return only about 30 members?

    Skool's public member page shows the first 30 rows. Separate admin and online tabs expose their first pages too, so memberScope: "all" merges and deduplicates all three tabs. Raise maxMembersPerCommunity to keep the full union. Full member rosters and member emails are not public.

    More from the publisher:

    • Why are members missing for some communities?
    • Why is a price or owner field missing on some records?

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.
