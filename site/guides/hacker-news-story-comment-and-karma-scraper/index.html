    Hacker News Story, Comment & Karma Scraper: export to CSV, Excel or JSON

    Pull Hacker News front-page, newest, best, Ask HN, Show HN, or job stories, look up specific items or user profiles, or run a full-text search across HN history. Filter by score, domain, date range, and comment count or author.

    Fix common problems

    Do I need a proxy?

    No. The actor connects directly and needs no proxy configuration.

    Why is a field missing from some records?

    Hacker News does not store that field for every item. For example, self posts have text but no link URL, and many users have no “about” text. Missing fields are omitted instead of filled with a placeholder.

    Why do comment scores never appear?

    Hacker News does not publish per-comment scores through its feeds, search, or HTML. Stories, polls, jobs, and poll options do have scores.

    More from the publisher:

    • Why did a search stop at 1,000 results?
    • Why is relevance order different when exhaustiveSearch is on?

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.
