    Google Maps Related Place & Competitor Finder: export to CSV, Excel or JSON

    Find the related and 'people also search for' places Google Maps shows on a business, hotel, or landmark page. Get each related place's name, rating, review count, category, coordinates, and image, linked back to the place you searched.

    This tutorial runs Google Maps Related Place & Competitor Finder through the Apify API and saves what it returns as a CSV file, an Excel workbook or JSON, keeping the 8 columns its publisher puts first: recordType, relationMode, relationQuery, relatedSectionType, rank, name, placeId and featureId.

    What people export it for

    • Local SEO and competitive analysis — compare the places Google associates with a client's listing, or the competitors around its pin.
    • Market and category research — expand a handful of seed businesses into a wider set of nearby places in the same category.
    • Lead and partner discovery — collect contacts, websites, and ratings for businesses around a target area.
    • Data enrichment — attach canonical Place IDs, categories, and coordinates to an existing list of businesses.
    • Recommendation feeds — build a "places like this" list from Google's own related signal plus a transparent fallback.

    Fix common problems

    Why did carousel mode return no rows?

    Google does not show a related-place carousel for many signed-out place pages. carousel is truthful about that; use auto or sameCategory when you need rows.

    Why is address or website missing from a carousel row?

    The carousel does not carry contacts. Turn on includeDetails to look up each place's own page; if Google still does not publish the field, it is omitted.

    Why does distanceMeters differ from the distance badge on the card?

    The badge is Google's own display string. distanceMeters is a straight-line calculation from the source coordinates to the related place coordinates.

    More from the publisher:

    • Why are prices in an unexpected currency?
    • Do I need a proxy or cookies?

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.
