    Fix common problems

    Why is listPrice missing on a product that shows a "Typical price"?

    "Typical price" is not a strike-through list price. It is returned separately as typicalPrice, and discountPercent is calculated only from a genuine current price plus list price. This prevents fake discounts.

    Why are Prime or sponsored fields missing from some records?

    Amazon does not render Prime icons or sponsored labels for every marketplace, exit country, or layout. The actor reports only what the page actually shows and never claims a product is "not Prime" from a missing badge.

    Why do I get fewer results than maxPages suggests?

    Amazon caps keyword search at page 7. Category listings can go deeper. For more than a keyword surface allows, split the query by category, brand, or price band and combine the runs.
