    SBA Certified Small Business & Federal Contractor Lookup export

    Search the SBA's public small business directory by name, state, NAICS code, or federal certification (8(a), HUBZone, WOSB, VOSB, SDVOSB) and get contact info, NAICS codes, and certification status.

    This tutorial runs SBA Certified Small Business & Federal Contractor Lookup through the Apify API and saves what it returns as a CSV file, an Excel workbook or JSON, keeping the 8 columns its publisher puts first: legalBusinessName, dbaName, city, state, primaryNaicsCode, phone, website and activeFederalCertifications.
