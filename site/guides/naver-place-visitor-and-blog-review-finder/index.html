    Fix common problems

    Why do some visitor reviews have no star rating?

    Naver returns individual star ratings only for its newest reviews on anonymous requests; older reviews in the same cursor stream come back with no rating. The actor omits the field rather than guessing. Text, dates, reviewer, tags, photos, and replies still fill normally, and the stats record's ratingDistribution covers every rating Naver reports for the place.

    Why is blogDate now filled?

    The blog/cafe review endpoint returns an exact publish date (YYYY.MM.DD.). The actor normalizes it to YYYY-MM-DD. Blog posts are still capped at 100 per place by Naver.

    Why are some stats fields missing for a place?

    Naver doesn't track an average rating, star distribution, themes, or menus for every vertical. Cafes and some clinics often have no average or histogram. Unavailable fields are omitted rather than guessed.

    More from the publisher:

    • Why might reviewPhotos be empty even with includeReviewPhotos enabled?
    • Do I need a proxy?

    HTTP 401 means the token is missing; an empty file usually means the input matched nothing, which the run log in Console names.
